2. **CSP** — Prevents XSS, whitelists resources

### 💡 Key Learnings

✅ **HTTPS/TLS:** Encrypts all traffic, authenticates server  
✅ **Load Balancer TLS Termination:** Efficient, scalable architecture  
✅ **HSTS:** Forces HTTPS for future visits, prevents downgrade  
✅ **CSP:** Whitelists resource sources, prevents XSS  
✅ **Security Headers:** Multiple layers of defense  
✅ **CORS is NOT Security:** Only browser-level courtesy, needs auth/authz
